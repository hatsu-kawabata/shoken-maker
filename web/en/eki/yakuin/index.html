Yakuin Station: Trade-Area Population & Age Structure

Residential population around Yakuin Station, aggregated from the 500m grid of the 2020 Population Census of Japan. About 65,911 passengers use the station daily (FY2023, MLIT). Within a 1 km radius there are about 69,554 residents in 49,056 households, the average age is 41.3, and 12.9% of residents are 65 or older. About 103,594 people work within the same 1 km radius (1.49x the resident population). Its 1 km population ranks 92 of 1,995 stations nationwide (top 5%).
