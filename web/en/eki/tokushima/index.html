Tokushima Station: Trade-Area Population & Age Structure

Residential population around Tokushima Station, aggregated from the 500m grid of the 2020 Population Census of Japan. About 13,004 passengers use the station daily (FY2023, MLIT). Within a 1 km radius there are about 13,735 residents in 7,402 households, the average age is 50.5, and 29.5% of residents are 65 or older. About 27,330 people work within the same 1 km radius (1.99x the resident population). Its 1 km population ranks 1,764 of 1,995 stations nationwide (bottom 12%).
