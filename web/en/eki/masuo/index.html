Masuo Station: Trade-Area Population & Age Structure

Residential population around Masuo Station, aggregated from the 500m grid of the 2020 Population Census of Japan. About 12,315 passengers use the station daily (FY2023, MLIT). Within a 1 km radius there are about 23,830 residents in 10,057 households, the average age is 50.0, and 32.7% of residents are 65 or older.
