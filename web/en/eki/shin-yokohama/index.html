Shin-Yokohama Station: Trade-Area Population & Age Structure

Residential population around Shin-Yokohama Station, aggregated from the 500m grid of the 2020 Population Census of Japan. About 358,093 passengers use the station daily (FY2023, MLIT). Within a 1 km radius there are about 30,460 residents in 16,472 households, the average age is 43.8, and 18.0% of residents are 65 or older. About 61,299 people work within the same 1 km radius (2.01x the resident population). Its 1 km population ranks 1,077 of 1,995 stations nationwide (bottom 46%).
