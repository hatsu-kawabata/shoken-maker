Matsusaka Station: Trade-Area Population & Age Structure

Residential population around Matsusaka Station, aggregated from the 500m grid of the 2020 Population Census of Japan. About 13,913 passengers use the station daily (FY2023, MLIT). Within a 1 km radius there are about 11,045 residents in 5,218 households, the average age is 51.5, and 34.2% of residents are 65 or older. About 12,880 people work within the same 1 km radius (1.17x the resident population). Its 1 km population ranks 1,863 of 1,995 stations nationwide (bottom 7%).
