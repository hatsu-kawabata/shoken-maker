Yasu Station: Trade-Area Population & Age Structure

Residential population around Yasu Station, aggregated from the 500m grid of the 2020 Population Census of Japan. About 27,190 passengers use the station daily (FY2023, MLIT). Within a 1 km radius there are about 15,285 residents in 6,963 households, the average age is 43.0, and 19.6% of residents are 65 or older. About 8,845 people work within the same 1 km radius (0.58x the resident population). Its 1 km population ranks 1,710 of 1,995 stations nationwide (bottom 14%).
