Sakura-Shinmachi Station: Trade-Area Population & Age Structure

Residential population around Sakura-Shinmachi Station, aggregated from the 500m grid of the 2020 Population Census of Japan. About 65,657 passengers use the station daily (FY2023, MLIT).
