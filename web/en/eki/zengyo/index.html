Zengyo Station: Trade-Area Population & Age Structure

Residential population around Zengyo Station, aggregated from the 500m grid of the 2020 Population Census of Japan. About 25,800 passengers use the station daily (FY2023, MLIT). Within a 1 km radius there are about 26,304 residents in 12,152 households, the average age is 47.9, and 27.9% of residents are 65 or older. About 5,257 people work within the same 1 km radius (0.20x the resident population). Its 1 km population ranks 1,266 of 1,995 stations nationwide (bottom 37%).
