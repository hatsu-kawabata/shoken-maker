Koshigaya Station: Trade-Area Population & Age Structure

Residential population around Koshigaya Station, aggregated from the 500m grid of the 2020 Population Census of Japan. About 47,032 passengers use the station daily (FY2023, MLIT). About 12,684 people work within the same 1 km radius (0.35x the resident population). Its 1 km population ranks 821 of 1,995 stations nationwide (top 41%).
