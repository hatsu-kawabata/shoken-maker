Oji Station: Trade-Area Population & Age Structure

Residential population around Oji Station, aggregated from the 500m grid of the 2020 Population Census of Japan. About 171,699 passengers use the station daily (FY2023, MLIT). Within a 1 km radius there are about 57,000 residents in 30,816 households, the average age is 46.2, and 25.2% of residents are 65 or older. About 28,527 people work within the same 1 km radius (0.50x the resident population). Its 1 km population ranks 278 of 1,995 stations nationwide (top 14%).
